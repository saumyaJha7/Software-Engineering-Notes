## Q. What we will do when we design a system ?

#### 1. break down ps into small solvable sub problems 
#### 2. decide on key components nd their responsibility
#### 3. decide on boundaries of each component ( no overlapping of responsibility )
#### 4. think about key challenges in scaling each component 
#### 5. make our architecture fault tolerant nd available
