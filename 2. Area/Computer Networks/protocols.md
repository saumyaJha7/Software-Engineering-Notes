## Q. What is protocol ?
### Ans : In computer networks, a **protocol** is a set of **rules and standards** that devices follow to communicate with each other , like [[IP Address|IP]]

- Just like humans need a **common language** to talk,
- Computers need a **protocol** to understand and exchange data correctly.


### **Real-life Analogy:**

Imagine you’re playing a game of cricket:
- Everyone follows the **same rules** (how to bat, how to bowl, what’s an out).
- If players followed different rules, the game wouldn’t work.

Similarly, in networking:
- Devices must follow **the same protocol** (rules) for communication to succeed.

### **Real-life Example (Simple):**

Think of **a phone call**:
- You **say hello** first (like a handshake in networking).
- You both **take turns speaking** (like data packets being sent).
- If someone doesn’t hear you, they say “Can you repeat?” (like error checking).
- You **end with goodbye** (closing the connection).

This is basically a **communication protocol** in human terms 😊

### A **protocol** = agreed-upon rules for communication.  
### Without it, computers would talk in different “languages” and never understand each other.
