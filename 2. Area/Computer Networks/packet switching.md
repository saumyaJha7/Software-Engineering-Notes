## Q. What is packet switching ?
### Ans : Data is broken into packets, and each packet is sent independently across the network. The packets may take different routes and are reassembled at the destination.


### How it works :- 

- Like sending letters: each packet has an address and can travel any available route.
- The network shares bandwidth dynamically among many users.


## Key Features :- 
### 1. Efficient use of bandwidth (resources shared among users).
### 2. Packets can arrive out of order, with delays or losses possible.
### 3. Suitable for bursty data traffic like internet browsing, emails, streaming.


## Main example is : The Internet (TCP/IP networks).


### **Quick Analogy:**
- **Circuit Switching** = A dedicated train track from city A to city B (only one train can use it during the trip).
- **Packet Switching** = Many cars (packets) on a road network, each finding its own path to reach the same destination.
