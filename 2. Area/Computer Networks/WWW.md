## Q. What is WWW ?
### Ans : It is a **system of interlinked documents (webpages) and resources** that you can access through the Internet using a **web browser** (like Chrome, Firefox). Each document is identified by a **URL (Uniform Resource Locator)**.




## Q. Advantages of  WWW ?
### 1. **Make information sharing easier** between scientists worldwide.
### 2. Enable **hyperlinks** → connecting one document to another across the globe.
